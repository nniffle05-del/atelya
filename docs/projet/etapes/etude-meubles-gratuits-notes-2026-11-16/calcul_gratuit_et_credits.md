> Method note. VERIFIED = I opened the official page or document myself on 16 Nov 2026 and the quoted words come from it. Some quotes are excerpts of longer sentences (the reading tool limits quote length; where a sentence was returned in two consecutive fragments I say so). Three sites cannot be read directly (kaggle.com is JavaScript-only, cloud.google.com/startup returns only its title, scaleway.com and ovhcloud.com price pages return HTTP 403 or are cut): for those I read the official page through a public text-rendering proxy (r.jina.ai) and say "via r.jina.ai" each time. Two official PDFs (EuroHPC terms of reference, NVIDIA API trial terms) were downloaded and their text extracted locally. UNVERIFIED = seen only in a search summary, forum, blog or aggregator.
>
> Scope note (changed by the coordinator during the run): the owner accepts RENTED cloud machines for production; what he refuses is paying per object or a subscription. >
> Hardware facts used but not taken from the pages read (general knowledge of the cards): Tesla T4 = 16 GB, P100 = 16 GB, V100 = 16 GB, V100S = 32 GB, Quadro M4000 = 8 GB.

## 1. First trial now (about 10 objects): Kaggle Notebooks

### Takeaway
Kaggle gives the most free GPU time (30 h/week, 2 x T4 or 1 x P100, 12 h sessions, background and scheduled runs) but only 16 GB cards, so only the 16 GB model (TRELLIS v1) can fit; and its Terms of Use restrict the Services to "internal, personal, non-commercial use", so a company trial, and above all clients' photos, are outside its terms.

## 3. First trial now: Hugging Face ZeroGPU and public demo Spaces

### Takeaway
Hugging Face is the free quota whose hardware fits all three models: ZeroGPU runs on NVIDIA RTX Pro 6000 Blackwell with 48 GB (default) or 96 GB; a free account gets 5 GPU-minutes per day (2 without account, 40 with the paid PRO plan); the official Microsoft TRELLIS.2 demo runs on it and Spaces are officially callable by program; a free account older than 30 days may host 2 ZeroGPU Spaces of its own. Limits: the tiny daily quota (10 objects = 2 to 10 days), no official SAM 3D Objects demo, and a public demo is a third party's server on US-located infrastructure, with a notice advising against private data.

### Cited Findings
- VERIFIED. Hardware: ZeroGPU "dynamically allocates and releases NVIDIA RTX Pro 6000 Blackwell GPUs as needed"; sizes: `large` (default) = "Half NVIDIA RTX Pro 6000 Blackwell", 48GB, quota cost 1x; `xlarge` = "Full NVIDIA RTX Pro 6000 Blackwell", 96GB, quota cost 2x — [HF ZeroGPU docs](https://huggingface.co/docs/hub/spaces-zerogpu)
- VERIFIED. Daily quotas: Unauthenticated 2 minutes (low priority); Free account 5 minutes (medium); PRO account 40 minutes (extensible, highest); Team member 40 minutes; Enterprise member 60 minutes. "Included daily quota resets exactly 24 hours after your first GPU usage." "Remaining quota directly impacts priority in ZeroGPU queues." — [HF ZeroGPU docs](https://huggingface.co/docs/hub/spaces-zerogpu)
- VERIFIED. Use is free: "ZeroGPU Spaces are available to use for free to all users." — [HF ZeroGPU docs](https://huggingface.co/docs/hub/spaces-zerogpu)
- VERIFIED. Hosting your own: "Free personal accounts: accounts in good standing (verified email, account older than 30 days) can host up to 2 ZeroGPU Spaces for free."; PRO: up to 10; Team & Enterprise organisations: up to 50 — [HF ZeroGPU docs](https://huggingface.co/docs/hub/spaces-zerogpu)
- VERIFIED. Going beyond the quota is reserved to paying plans: "PRO, Team, and Enterprise users can continue using ZeroGPU Spaces beyond their included daily quota by consuming pre-paid credits at the rate of **$1 per 10 minutes** of GPU time." — [HF ZeroGPU docs](https://huggingface.co/docs/hub/spaces-zerogpu)

- VERIFIED. HF Terms of Service ("Effective Date: September 15, 2022") contain no clause forbidding commercial use and none on automated calls; they do say "we reserve the right to suspend or terminate your access to the Services anytime with or without cause" and "We may at any time modify, suspend, or discontinue, temporarily or permanently, the Services" — [HF Terms of Service](https://huggingface.co/terms-of-service)

### Inferences
- Capacity with ONE free account on the official TRELLIS.2 demo: 300 GPU-seconds per day; one object = two GPU steps, each reserving up to 120 s. If real run time is charged: about 3-5 objects/day. If reserved time is charged (forum inference): 2 calls/day = 1 object/day. So 10 objects = 2 to 10 days, at zero cost. A private copy with a shorter declared duration would use the quota better.
- A private copy of the Space on the company's own free account (allowed: 2 ZeroGPU Spaces, account older than 30 days) removes Microsoft's server code and the extra call to the BRIA Space, but the same 5 minutes/day applies and it is still Hugging Face's infrastructure.
- SAM 3D Objects (32 GB) fits the 48 GB slice in principle but has no official demo: it needs the company's own ZeroGPU Space and acceptance of Meta's gated licence.
- The demo's own notice ("advised against uploading data containing sensitive or private information") plus the absence of any data-processing agreement mean: trial with NON-client pictures only.
- Not a production component: one apartment (10-30 objects) already exceeds a day's free quota, and Hugging Face may change or stop the service at any time.

### Gaps
- Official accounting rule (declared vs real duration; refusal when remaining quota is below the declared duration; any cap on the number of runs) not found on an official page.
- The official demo relies on the BRIA RMBG-2.0 background-removal Space; that model's licence (from memory: non-commercial unless agreed with BRIA) was NOT checked during this research and should be read before any company use of the demo as is.
- Whether Hugging Face logs Space inputs beyond the session is not described in the Privacy Policy read.

## 5. Credit programmes open to a French early-stage company WITHOUT venture capital: Microsoft, Google, AWS, NVIDIA

### Takeaway
Without an investor, the realistic US-cloud grants are: Microsoft for Startups $200 at sign-up then $5,000 after business verification (180 days; more only with sustained use of 5+ Azure services), AWS Activate Founders $1,000 (up to $5,000 for some), Google "Start" tier $2,000 (company under 2 years with an MVP). All are meant for building and running a commercial product, all need a payment card and fall back to normal billing when credits end, and GPU machines are NOT available by default on any of them (quota 0, increase on request, refusals reported). NVIDIA Inception gives no compute by itself.

### Inferences
- GDPR: free notebooks, public demos and trial APIs offer no data-processing agreement and several explicitly ask not to send private data: clients' photos of their homes must not go there. Trial = the company's own or licensed test pictures. Production = a rented machine under a contract that includes a data-processing agreement, preferably in the EU (OVHcloud, Scaleway, or an EU region of a US cloud).
- Dependence: a sold product must not rest on a free quota (5 minutes a day, changeable overnight). It can rest on rented machines paid by credits for 6-12 months, because the price after the credits is known and small (section 7).
- Using several accounts to multiply free quotas is explicitly forbidden by Colab and is the classic reason for suspension elsewhere.

## 9. Bottom line: ranking and compact table

### Takeaway
(a) First trial of about 10 objects, at zero cost, with non-client pictures: Hugging Face ZeroGPU first (official TRELLIS.2 demo or a private copy; 48 GB; 2-10 days with one free account), then Lightning AI's one-off free credits for a real 24-48 GB machine; Colab and Kaggle only reach the 16 GB model and Kaggle's terms exclude companies. (b) Free rented machines at launch: OVHcloud Start (EUR 10,000, 12 months, EU, but V100S 32 GB only) and Scaleway Founders (EUR 1,000, any GPU) first, then Microsoft ($5,000), Google ($2,000) and AWS ($1,000) if their GPU quota is granted; each covers from several hundred to several thousand apartments. Public supercomputers are for R&D projects, not for running the service.

### Inferences
Ranking for the trial (about 10 objects):
1. Hugging Face ZeroGPU — only free quota with 48 GB; official TRELLIS.2 demo; program calls allowed; non-client pictures only.

6. Kaggle — 16 GB, TRELLIS v1 only; terms: personal, non-commercial.
7. EuroHPC Playground — free and fast to obtain, but an application file and reporting duties: worth it for a larger R&D campaign, heavy for 10 objects.
